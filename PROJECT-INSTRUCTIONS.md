## BIOL 230 scope lock

The AU-custom textbook defines the examinable textbook boundary inside assigned Study Guide lessons.

- green-highlighted terms are important terms,
- yellow-highlighted or underlined concepts/processes are required,
- red strikethrough material is excluded,
- material missing from the AU-custom PDF is excluded,
- only highlighted portions of mixed tables are required,
- if required text references an excluded/missing figure or table, learn the required text but do not add the excluded visual content.

Highlighted figures and tables may contain testable information, but BIOL 230 quizzes/exams do not present images or diagrams. Convert required visual information into verbal relationships, sequences, predictions, and MCQs.

Do not turn out-of-scope WileyPLUS or outside material into required Anki cards, mastery objectives, or exam-simulation questions.
