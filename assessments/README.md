## Weekly assessment principles

Closed-book, usually 60–90 minutes.

Sample:
- current material,
- older material,
- mechanisms,
- prediction/perturbation,
- graphs/data/calculations where in scope,
- integration,
- brief Feynman explanation.

Approximate weekly distribution:
- 40% current week
- 40% earlier material
- 20% integration/transfer

As an AU exam approaches, increase emphasis on the current non-cumulative exam block.

## BIOL 230 simulation

Real quizzes:
- 50 MCQ / 60 min.

Real exams:
- 100 MCQ / 120 min.

Simulation questions should stay within AU-defined scope, use straightforward one-best-answer wording, and avoid invented trick-question style.

Historical snapshots are evidence and should not be rewritten after finalization.
