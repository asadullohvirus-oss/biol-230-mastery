## Card admission rules

Create a card only when at least one applies:
- high-yield physiological relationship,
- recurring error,
- equation/relationship requiring automatic recall,
- commonly confused comparison,
- essential terminology needed for reasoning,
- frequently required physiological chain,
- retrieval failure blocks later understanding.

Prefer:
- prediction cards,
- perturbation cards,
- mechanism completion,
- compare/contrast,
- equations,
- mistake-correction cards.

Avoid:
- every highlighted word,
- rare/out-of-scope facts,
- giant backs,
- cards testing multiple unrelated facts,
- content excluded by the AU-custom PDF.

## Load

Starting target: roughly 8–15 high-value new cards on a normal study day.
Hard ceiling around 20 unless justified.
Reduce new cards if due reviews repeatedly consume more than about 30 minutes/day.
