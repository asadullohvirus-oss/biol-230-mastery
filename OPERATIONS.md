## Assignment workflow

For Assignments 1–3:
- inspect relevant questions while progressing through chapters,
- build conceptual preparation incrementally,
- write original answers,
- submit .docx through the correct Brightspace assignment page,
- save submission confirmation,
- review instructor feedback when marked.

## Quiz feedback workflow

Instructor feedback is expected by email within eight business days after a quiz and identifies the textbook learning objective related to each incorrect response.

On feedback receipt:
1. capture the missed objective,
2. diagnose the error,
3. remediate,
4. retest,
5. update MASTERY/MISTAKES if warranted.
