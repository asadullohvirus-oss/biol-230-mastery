## Compression reality

Our target compresses roughly 47 weeks of suggested activity into about 9 weeks.

AU estimates approximately 5–8 hours/week across the normal 52-week course contract. A straight-line conversion would imply a very large weekly workload under our timeline, so 25 hours/week should not be treated as sufficient by default.

Initial capacity plan:
- reserve approximately 30–40 focused hours/week during the accelerated sprint,
- use the first 7 days to measure actual hours per chapter, quiz preparation, and Assignment 1 work,
- reforecast immediately from real data,
- protect sleep and accuracy rather than using low-quality exhausted hours.

The system should reduce administrative overhead before reducing retrieval, graded-work quality, or sleep.
